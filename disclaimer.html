⚠️ Important Notice Regarding Donations

TempleDiary.in is a purely community-driven, non-commercial initiative. We do not accept any donations, fees, or money transactions — now or in the future. This is, and will always remain, a completely free platform for devotees.

If anyone approaches you asking for money, donations, or "registration fees" in the name of TempleDiary, please do not entertain them — they are not associated with us.

1. How We Are Funded

To keep the servers running and the platform free for everyone, the site is sustained solely through standard website advertisements (e.g., Google AdSense). We do not solicit financial contributions from users or temples.

2. The Pauranik Welfare Pledge

As part of our commitment to Sanatana Dharma, a part of any net ad revenue generated by this site goes directly to the welfare of elderly Pauraniks, Archakas, and lifelong sevaks of small, underfunded temples. This is our moral pledge, funded entirely by the site's own ad revenue — not by user donations.

3. Accuracy of Information

TempleDiary aggregates data from public domain sources, Devaswom boards, and community submissions. While we strive to verify all listings, we cannot guarantee 100% accuracy at all times regarding temple timings, contact numbers, or specific pooja details. Devotees are encouraged to verify critical information directly with the temple authorities before making travel arrangements.

4. Community Contributions

Any historical information, legends, or descriptions submitted by the community are published in good faith to preserve heritage. TempleDiary does not claim to be an authoritative theological or historical source.

If you have questions or wish to report incorrect information, please use our Contact Form.
